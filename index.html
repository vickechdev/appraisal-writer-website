FAQ

Questions appraisers ask us

Who signs the report?

You do — always. We prepare the report; the valuation opinion, final adjustments, reconciliation and signature remain yours as the licensed appraiser.

Do you work directly in ACI?

Yes. ACI is our primary platform, and we return native files. We also work in TOTAL/WinTOTAL, ClickFORMS, Appraise-It and Narrative1.

What should I upload?

Engagement letter, inspection notes (any format), photos, sketch measurements, your selected MLS comps and any prior workfile. If something's missing, we'll ask.

How is my data protected?

Files are used only to prepare your report and are never sold or shared. We follow confidentiality protocols consistent with the USPAP Ethics Rule and are happy to sign an NDA before your first order. See our .

How does the free trial report work?

Send one standard residential assignment. We prepare it at no charge so you can judge quality and fit before committing to anything.
